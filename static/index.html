👋 Welcome to Coach Potato! Add your Riot API key and the accounts to track, save, then hit Update data to fetch your match history (a long first crawl — roughly 2 minutes per 100 games).

Riot API & accounts

Get one at developer.riotgames.com. Best option: register a personal product there for a persistent key. The instant development key works too, but expires every 24 h — paste a fresh one here when crawls start failing.

Any number of Riot IDs, e.g. Player#EUW. Stats combine all of them.

While the app is open, new games are fetched on this schedule (0 = off). Data also updates once on startup and whenever you press Update data.

Blocks

Blocks auto-advance after this many games. Changing it applies to the current and future blocks; finished blocks keep their size.

Blocks are meant to be played in succession: if a game you add starts this many hours (game time) away from the block's latest game, the block closes and a new one starts. 0 turns this off.

Untick to auto-close silently when the time gap is exceeded.

A series (e.g. a 2-week challenge) gives your blocks a shared title with a per-series number after it, and a "+ New series" button on the Blocks page. Untick to just number blocks continuously as "#1, #2, …".

Playbook

"Per matchup" keeps rune pages inside each opponent's row. "General" shows a single champion-level rune set up next to the item build instead. Your per-matchup rune pages aren't deleted — they reappear if you switch back.

Research & comparison

Add up to five other players (looked up via the Riot API) to compare yourself against in a matchup — their stats, runes and recent games show side by side with yours in the Playbook, for theorycrafting runes and notes. Games are fetched deep in the background when you add a player (Riot's API is slow, so it can take a few minutes); "Fetch more" pulls further back. The guide comparison shows every stored game, regardless of date.

The players themselves are managed on their own page under Prepare → .

Champion pool

Your standing commitment: what you blind pick, what's in your core pool, and what you keep for counters. Drag chips to reorder — the first champion is your main, and becomes the default in the Playbook and the cooldown comparison. Your pool champions are also listed first, under a "Champion Pool" heading, in champion pickers across the app, and the whole pool is stamped onto each block when it completes. Changes save when you press "Save pool".
